Keep StrictModes enabled. SubPath mounts do not refresh on Secret updates; replace/restart the corresponding runner after authorized-key rotation and verify authentication. Obtain each **public** host key directly through the trusted Kubernetes control channel and compare its fingerprint; build a bracketed `[runner-PROVIDER.paperclip-eval.svc.cluster.local]:2222` known_hosts line.
